# 02 — Banned AI uses (AI Act Art 5)

Screen every PRD. The law: [AI Act Art 5](../ai-act/ch-02_prohibited-practices/art-005.md). Its footer maps each point to the Commission's guidelines. Those guidelines date from July 2025 and say nothing on points (ba), (bb), (1a) or (1b).

| If the PRD… | Point | Guidelines |
|---|---|---|
| steers behaviour by tricks people can't see, or by deception, in a way likely to cause significant harm | 5(1)(a) | [§3.2.1](../guidelines-art5/03_manipulation-and-exploitation/3-2-1.md), [§3.2.2](../guidelines-art5/03_manipulation-and-exploitation/3-2-2.md), [§3.2.3](../guidelines-art5/03_manipulation-and-exploitation/3-2-3.md) |
| exploits age, disability or social or economic situation to distort behaviour, likely causing significant harm | 5(1)(b) | [§3.3.1](../guidelines-art5/03_manipulation-and-exploitation/3-3-1.md), [§3.3.2](../guidelines-art5/03_manipulation-and-exploitation/3-3-2.md), [§3.3.3](../guidelines-art5/03_manipulation-and-exploitation/3-3-3.md) |
| makes sexual images or video of an identifiable real person without their explicit consent | 5(1)(ba), with 5(1a), 5(1b) | none |
| makes child sexual abuse material | 5(1)(bb), with 5(1a) | none |
| scores people on behaviour or traits, leading to unfair treatment elsewhere | 5(1)(c) | [§4](../guidelines-art5/04_social-scoring/CONTEXT.md) |
| predicts someone will commit a crime from profiling alone | 5(1)(d) | [§5](../guidelines-art5/05_crime-risk-prediction/CONTEXT.md) |
| scrapes face images from the internet or CCTV to build face databases | 5(1)(e) | [§6](../guidelines-art5/06_facial-image-scraping/CONTEXT.md) |
| reads emotions at work or in school (medical and safety uses aside) | 5(1)(f) | [§7](../guidelines-art5/07_emotion-recognition/CONTEXT.md) |
| sorts people by biometrics to infer race, politics, union membership, religion, sex life or orientation | 5(1)(g) | [§8](../guidelines-art5/08_biometric-categorisation/CONTEXT.md) |
| identifies people by face etc. in real time in public, for police | 5(1)(h), 5(2)–(7) | [§9](../guidelines-art5/09_real-time-remote-biometric-identification/CONTEXT.md), [§10](../guidelines-art5/10_biometric-identification-safeguards/CONTEXT.md) |

## Watch
- Each point has conditions; a match on the topic alone is **decide**, not **breaks**. The guidelines' "Out of scope" sections list what falls outside: [§3.5](../guidelines-art5/03_manipulation-and-exploitation/3-5.md), [§4.3](../guidelines-art5/04_social-scoring/4-3.md), [§7.4](../guidelines-art5/07_emotion-recognition/7-4.md), [§8.3](../guidelines-art5/08_biometric-categorisation/8-3.md).
- (ba) and (bb) reach a general image or video generator too, not only one built for such content: [Art 5(1a)(a)(ii)](../ai-act/ch-02_prohibited-practices/art-005.md) bans release where the output is a foreseeable result and there are no adequate safeguards. Any PRD that generates images of real people should state its safeguards.
- Dates: [timeline.md](timeline.md). The guidelines don't bind: [para 5](../guidelines-art5/01_background.md).

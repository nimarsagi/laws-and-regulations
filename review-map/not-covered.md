# Not in this library

The library holds three texts: the AI Act (as amended in 2026), the GDPR, and the Commission's Art 5 guidelines. When a PRD touches anything below, list it under **Not checked** in the report; don't answer it from memory.

## Other EU law a PRD often touches
- Cookies, tracking, direct marketing by email or SMS: ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC and national rules.
- Unfair or misleading selling to consumers, dark patterns: Unfair Commercial Practices Directive, other consumer law.
- Online platforms, marketplaces, content moderation: Digital Services Act.
- Training on or generating copyrighted material, text and data mining opt-outs: Copyright Directive (EU) 2019/790. The AI Act only points to it (Art 53(1)(c)).
- Data from connected devices: Data Act. Product defects and harm: Product Liability Directive. Security of digital products: Cyber Resilience Act. Security duties for certain sectors: NIS2.
- Accessibility: European Accessibility Act.
- Sector rules: medical devices, financial services, insurance, employment, education, children's online safety.

## National law
- Choices the GDPR leaves to each country: the age of digital consent (Art 8), staff data (Art 88), and more.
- Countries outside the EU, such as New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020. The EU laws here still reach a non-EU business if [01](01_scope-and-roles.md) says they do.

## Supporting material
- AI Act recitals (the Art 5 guidelines cite them; their links show as "not in this library").
- Other Commission guidelines (the AI system definition, high-risk classification), codes of practice, standards, templates.
- Data protection board (EDPB) guidance, court rulings, GDPR corrections published after 2016, the current list of adequacy decisions.

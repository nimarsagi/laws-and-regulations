# 04 — Telling people it's AI, and AI literacy

## Transparency — [AI Act Art 50](../ai-act/ch-04_transparency-obligations/art-050.md)
Applies whether or not the system is high-risk.
1. **Talks to people?** The provider must make sure people know they're dealing with AI, unless it's obvious: 50(1).
2. **Generates images, audio, video or text?** The provider must mark outputs in a machine-readable way as AI-made: 50(2). Generators already on the market before 2 Aug 2026 have until 2 Dec 2026: [Art 111(4)](../ai-act/ch-13_final-provisions/art-111.md).
3. **Emotion recognition or biometric sorting?** The deployer must tell the people exposed: 50(3). Check [02](02_prohibited-practices.md) first.
4. **Deepfakes, or AI text published to inform the public?** The deployer must disclose it: 50(4). Definition of deepfake: [Art 3(60)](../ai-act/ch-01_general-provisions/art-003.md). Openly artistic, satirical or fictional work only needs a light disclosure: same paragraph.
5. **When and how:** clearly, at the first interaction or exposure at the latest: 50(5).

A PRD that builds on someone else's model is usually the provider of its own system for 50(1) and 50(2), not only a user of the model: see [01](01_scope-and-roles.md) q4.

## AI literacy — [AI Act Art 4](../ai-act/ch-01_general-provisions/art-004.md)
Rewritten in 2026: providers and deployers must "take measures to support the development of AI literacy" of staff and others using AI for them, suited to their knowledge and to whom the AI is used on. It doesn't require any set level. Relevant to any PRD that rolls AI out to staff, or writes an organisation's AI-use rules.

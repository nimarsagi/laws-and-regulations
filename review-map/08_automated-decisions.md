# 08 — Decisions made by AI about people

For PRDs where the AI's output decides, or shapes a decision, about a person.

1. **Decided by the machine alone?** [GDPR Art 22](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-022.md): people may refuse decisions based solely on automated processing, profiling included, with legal or similarly significant effects. Allowed only for a contract, by law, or with explicit consent (22(2)), and then with a right to a human, to give their view, and to contest (22(3)). Sensitive data: 22(4). Examples and the children point: [Recital 71](../gdpr/recitals/rec-071.md).
2. **Is the human review real?** A person who rubber-stamps doesn't take it outside Art 22. The PRD should say who reviews, with what authority. For high-risk AI: [AI Act Art 14](../ai-act/ch-03_high-risk-ai-systems/art-014.md) (oversight built in), [Art 26(2)](../ai-act/ch-03_high-risk-ai-systems/art-026.md) (named, trained people).
3. **Explanation** — people affected by a decision based on an Annex III high-risk system's output may ask the deployer for a clear explanation: [AI Act Art 86](../ai-act/ch-09_monitoring-enforcement-remedies/art-086.md). They must be told the system is used: [Art 26(11)](../ai-act/ch-03_high-risk-ai-systems/art-026.md).
4. **Told up front?** [GDPR Art 13(2)(f)](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-013.md), [Art 14(2)(g)](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-014.md), and on request [Art 15(1)(h)](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-015.md).

## Watch
- "Profiling" is defined in [GDPR Art 4(4)](../gdpr/ch-01_general-provisions/art-004.md); the AI Act uses the same meaning ([Art 3(52)](../ai-act/ch-01_general-provisions/art-003.md)). Profiling keeps an Annex III system high-risk: [03](03_high-risk.md).

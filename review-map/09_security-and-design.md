# 09 — Security, privacy by design, breaches

1. **Built in from the start** — [GDPR Art 25](../gdpr/ch-04_controller-and-processor/art-025.md), [Recital 78](../gdpr/recitals/rec-078.md): default settings collect and keep the least data, and share nothing without the person acting. Does the PRD collect, keep or share more than its steps need?
2. **Security** — [Art 32](../gdpr/ch-04_controller-and-processor/art-032.md), [Recital 83](../gdpr/recitals/rec-083.md): measures that fit the risk, such as encryption, pseudonymisation, access control, backups, testing. Who can open the stored files, and where are they kept?
3. **Breaches** — tell the supervisory authority within 72 hours unless unlikely to harm anyone: [Art 33](../gdpr/ch-04_controller-and-processor/art-033.md); tell the people affected when the risk is high: [Art 34](../gdpr/ch-04_controller-and-processor/art-034.md). A PRD that stores personal data should name who acts on a breach.
4. **High-risk AI** — accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity, including against poisoned data and manipulated inputs: [AI Act Art 15](../ai-act/ch-03_high-risk-ai-systems/art-015.md). Serious incidents: [Art 73](../ai-act/ch-09_monitoring-enforcement-remedies/art-073.md).

## Watch
- Content fed to a model from outside (a transcript, an email, a web page) can carry instructions. For high-risk systems, Art 15(5) names inputs designed to make the model err ("adversarial examples"); for others, guarding against this falls under Art 32's "appropriate" measures.

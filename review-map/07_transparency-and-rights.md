# 07 — Telling people, and their rights (GDPR)

1. **What people must be told** — collected from them: [GDPR Art 13](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-013.md); from elsewhere (a transcript, a scraped page, a shared file): [Art 14](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-014.md). Both require saying when automated decisions happen and how they work (13(2)(f), 14(2)(g)). Does the PRD have a notice step, and who shows it?
2. **How to tell them** — concise, plain, easy to reach; extra care for children: [Art 12(1)](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-012.md), [Recital 58](../gdpr/recitals/rec-058.md). Answer requests within a month: 12(3).
3. **Rights the PRD must be able to honour** — access [Art 15](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-015.md) · correction [Art 16](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-016.md) · erasure [Art 17](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-017.md) · restriction [Art 18](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-018.md) · portability [Art 20](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-020.md) · objection [Art 21](../gdpr/ch-03_data-subject-rights/art-021.md). For each store the PRD creates (records, registers, summaries), can one person's data be found, exported and deleted?

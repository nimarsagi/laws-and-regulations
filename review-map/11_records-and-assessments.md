# 11 — Records, impact assessments, data protection officer

1. **Record of processing** — [GDPR Art 30](../gdpr/ch-04_controller-and-processor/art-030.md): controllers and processors keep one. Organisations under 250 staff are spared unless the processing is risky, not occasional, or includes sensitive data (30(5)).
2. **Data protection impact assessment (DPIA)** — [Art 35](../gdpr/ch-04_controller-and-processor/art-035.md): required before processing likely to be high-risk, including (35(3)) automated evaluation with significant effects, large-scale sensitive data, and large-scale monitoring of public places. Read with [Recitals 84](../gdpr/recitals/rec-084.md), [89](../gdpr/recitals/rec-089.md), [90](../gdpr/recitals/rec-090.md), [91](../gdpr/recitals/rec-091.md). New technology such as AI is one of the risk signs (35(1)). If high risk remains after the DPIA, consult the authority first: [Art 36](../gdpr/ch-04_controller-and-processor/art-036.md).
3. **Data protection officer** — [Art 37](../gdpr/ch-04_controller-and-processor/art-037.md): needed for public bodies, large-scale regular monitoring, or large-scale sensitive data. Role: [Art 38](../gdpr/ch-04_controller-and-processor/art-038.md), [Art 39](../gdpr/ch-04_controller-and-processor/art-039.md).
4. **Fundamental rights impact assessment** — for some deployers of high-risk AI: [AI Act Art 27](../ai-act/ch-03_high-risk-ai-systems/art-027.md). May point to the DPIA instead of repeating it: 27(4), new in 2026.
5. **Registration of high-risk AI** — [AI Act Art 49](../ai-act/ch-03_high-risk-ai-systems/art-049.md), content in [Annex VIII](../ai-act/annexes/annex-08.md) (parts deleted in 2026).

## Watch
- A PRD whose steps include an AI evaluating people, or sensitive data, should have a DPIA step, or say why none is needed.

# 13 — Fines and liability

Use to say how much a finding matters, not to decide whether it's a finding. Caps are "whichever is higher" for most firms; for SMEs and start-ups "whichever is lower".

## AI Act — [Art 99](../ai-act/ch-12_penalties/art-099.md)
- Banned practices (Art 5): up to EUR 35m or 7% of worldwide yearly turnover: 99(3).
- Most other duties (providers, deployers, Art 50 transparency, and since 2026 Art 25(2) and (4)): up to EUR 15m or 3%: 99(4).
- Wrong or misleading information to authorities: up to EUR 7.5m or 1%: 99(5).
- SMEs: the lower figure: 99(6). Small mid-caps (SMC, new in 2026): the lower figure for 99(4) and (5): 99(6a).
- Model providers: up to EUR 15m or 3%, set by the Commission: [Art 101](../ai-act/ch-12_penalties/art-101.md). EU bodies: [Art 100](../ai-act/ch-12_penalties/art-100.md).

## GDPR — [Art 83](../gdpr/ch-08_remedies-liability-penalties/art-083.md)
- Controller and processor duties (Art 8, 11, 25–39 and others): up to EUR 10m or 2%: 83(4).
- Principles, legal basis, consent, sensitive data, people's rights, transfers (Art 5–7, 9, 12–22, 44–49): up to EUR 20m or 4%: 83(5).
- How fines are weighed: 83(2). People can claim damages: [Art 82](../gdpr/ch-08_remedies-liability-penalties/art-082.md). National penalties on top: [Art 84](../gdpr/ch-08_remedies-liability-penalties/art-084.md).

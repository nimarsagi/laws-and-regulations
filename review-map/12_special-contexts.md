# 12 — Children, health and disability, work, research

## Children
- Online services offered directly to a child relying on consent: parental consent below 16, or a lower national age not under 13: [GDPR Art 8](../gdpr/ch-02_principles/art-008.md). Why children get extra protection: [Recital 38](../gdpr/recitals/rec-038.md). Notices a child can understand: [Recital 58](../gdpr/recitals/rec-058.md). Solely automated decisions "should not concern a child": [Recital 71](../gdpr/recitals/rec-071.md).
- Exploiting a child's age to distort behaviour is banned: [AI Act Art 5(1)(b)](../ai-act/ch-02_prohibited-practices/art-005.md), [Guidelines §3.3.1](../guidelines-art5/03_manipulation-and-exploitation/3-3-1.md). High-risk providers must consider under-18s: [Art 9(9)](../ai-act/ch-03_high-risk-ai-systems/art-009.md). School uses (admission, grading, proctoring) are high-risk: [Annex III point 3](../ai-act/annexes/annex-03.md). Emotion reading in school is banned: [02](02_prohibited-practices.md).

## Health and disability
- Health data is sensitive: [GDPR Art 4(15)](../gdpr/ch-01_general-provisions/art-004.md), [Recital 35](../gdpr/recitals/rec-035.md), [Art 9](../gdpr/ch-02_principles/art-009.md). A note that someone has a disability or a condition is health data. Health-purpose exceptions: [Recital 53](../gdpr/recitals/rec-053.md).
- Exploiting disability is banned on the same terms as age: [AI Act Art 5(1)(b)](../ai-act/ch-02_prohibited-practices/art-005.md).

## Work
- Hiring, promotion, task allocation and staff monitoring are high-risk: [Annex III point 4](../ai-act/annexes/annex-03.md). Workers and their representatives told before use: [AI Act Art 26(7)](../ai-act/ch-03_high-risk-ai-systems/art-026.md). Emotion reading at work is banned: [02](02_prohibited-practices.md).
- National law and collective agreements may add rules for staff data: [GDPR Art 88](../gdpr/ch-09_specific-processing-situations/art-088.md), [Recital 155](../gdpr/recitals/rec-155.md). Consent is rarely free between employer and employee: [Recital 43](../gdpr/recitals/rec-043.md).

## Research
- GDPR allows research with safeguards such as pseudonymisation: [Art 89](../gdpr/ch-09_specific-processing-situations/art-089.md), [Recitals 157](../gdpr/recitals/rec-157.md), [159](../gdpr/recitals/rec-159.md). Research as a compatible later purpose: [Art 5(1)(b)](../gdpr/ch-02_principles/art-005.md).
- AI Act exclusions for research and pre-release testing: [01](01_scope-and-roles.md) q3. Testing in real conditions: [Art 60](../ai-act/ch-06_innovation-support/art-060.md), [Art 61](../ai-act/ch-06_innovation-support/art-061.md).

# 06 — Personal data: legal basis and principles (GDPR)

For any PRD handling information about identifiable people, once [01](01_scope-and-roles.md) says the GDPR applies.

1. **Principles** — [GDPR Art 5](../gdpr/ch-02_principles/art-005.md): a stated purpose, no more data than needed, accurate, kept no longer than needed, secure, and the controller must be able to show all this (5(2)). Check the PRD names its purpose and a retention period for each kind of data it keeps (recordings, transcripts, records).
2. **Legal basis** — [Art 6(1)](../gdpr/ch-02_principles/art-006.md): one of consent, contract, legal duty, vital interests, public task, legitimate interests. For legitimate interests, the PRD should show the balancing: [Recital 47](../gdpr/recitals/rec-047.md). Reuse for a new purpose: [Art 6(4)](../gdpr/ch-02_principles/art-006.md).
3. **Consent, if relied on** — [Art 7](../gdpr/ch-02_principles/art-007.md), [Art 4(11)](../gdpr/ch-01_general-provisions/art-004.md), [Recitals 32](../gdpr/recitals/rec-032.md), [42](../gdpr/recitals/rec-042.md), [43](../gdpr/recitals/rec-043.md): freely given, specific, informed, a clear act, as easy to withdraw as to give, and provable. Children: [12](12_special-contexts.md).
4. **Sensitive data** — [Art 9](../gdpr/ch-02_principles/art-009.md): health, biometrics used to identify, ethnicity, religion, politics, union, sex life or orientation, genetics. Banned unless one of 9(2)'s exceptions applies, usually explicit consent (9(2)(a)). Why: [Recital 51](../gdpr/recitals/rec-051.md). Crime data: [Art 10](../gdpr/ch-02_principles/art-010.md).
5. **Bias testing with sensitive data** — high-risk AI providers, and in narrower cases others, may use it under strict conditions: [AI Act Art 4a](../ai-act/ch-01_general-provisions/art-004a.md) (new in 2026).

## Watch
- Recordings and transcripts of calls hold personal data of everyone on them, not only the user's.
- Anonymised data falls outside the GDPR; pseudonymised data doesn't ([Art 4(5)](../gdpr/ch-01_general-provisions/art-004.md)).

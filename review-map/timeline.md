# When each rule applies

Compare each date with today and with the PRD's launch. A rule that isn't in force yet is still a finding if it will be by launch or soon after; say so and give the date.

## AI Act — [Art 113](../ai-act/ch-13_final-provisions/art-113.md), [Art 111](../ai-act/ch-13_final-provisions/art-111.md)
| From | What |
|---|---|
| 2 Feb 2025 | Chapters I–II: definitions, AI literacy (Art 4), banned practices (Art 5) |
| 2 Aug 2025 | Model providers (Chapter V), notified bodies (Ch III Section 4), governance (Ch VII), penalties (Ch XII except Art 101), Art 78 |
| 27 Jul 2026 | Articles 102–110 |
| 2 Aug 2026 | Everything else not listed here, including transparency (Art 50) and model-provider fines (Art 101) |
| 2 Dec 2026 | New bans 5(1)(ba), 5(1)(bb), 5(1a), 5(1b); content generators already on the market before 2 Aug 2026 must meet Art 50(2) marking (111(4)) |
| 2 Aug 2027 | Models already on the market before 2 Aug 2025 must comply (111(3)) |
| 2 Dec 2027 | High-risk duties (Ch III Sections 1–3) for Annex III systems |
| 2 Aug 2028 | High-risk duties for Annex I systems (safety components of regulated products) |
| 2 Aug 2030 | High-risk systems used by public authorities, even if already on the market (111(2)) |
| 31 Dec 2030 | AI in the EU's large IT systems listed in Annex X (111(1)) |

- High-risk systems already on the market before their date are covered only once their design changes significantly (111(2)).
- Commission documents still to come: Art 6(5) high-risk guidelines (were due 2 Feb 2026), Art 2(13) delegated acts (by 2 Aug 2027), Art 72(3) monitoring-plan template (by 2 Sep 2027). None are in this library.

## GDPR
Applies from 25 May 2018: [Art 99](../gdpr/ch-11_final-provisions/art-099.md). All of it is in force.

## Versions in this library
AI Act as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 of 8 July 2026 (OJ 24.7.2026), consolidated 27.07.2026. GDPR as published in 2016. Details: [_sources/CONTEXT.md](../_sources/CONTEXT.md).

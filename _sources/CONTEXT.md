# _sources — the original texts

The three files everything else is built from. Never edit them; to update a law, replace its file with the newer text and rerun `python3 _system/build.py`.

- `ai-act_consolidated_2026-07-27.md` — AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, EUR-Lex consolidated text 02024R1689 of 27.07.2026. Includes one amendment, M1: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 of 8 July 2026 (OJ 24.7.2026). EUR-Lex notes a consolidated text has no legal effect; the Official Journal versions do. No recitals.
- `gdpr_2016-679.md` — GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679, as published in OJ L 119, 4.5.2016. Articles and all 173 recitals. Later corrections are not included.
- `guidelines_prohibited-ai-practices_2025.md` — Commission guidelines on AI Act Article 5, C(2025) 5052 final, 29.7.2025. Not binding (para 5). Written before M1.
